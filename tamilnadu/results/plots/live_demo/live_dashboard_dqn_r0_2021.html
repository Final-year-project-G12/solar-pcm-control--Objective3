How to read this page

The controller picks one of three commands every hour: CHARGE (allow the pump), IDLE (pump off) or BYPASS (isolate the collector). CHARGE only means "pump allowed". The simulator itself switches the pump off whenever the collector would lose heat — all night and in weak sun — so a CHARGE command at night changes nothing. That is why this page colours each hour by what the pump physically did, not just by the command: otherwise most of the strip would look like "always charge".

Why so few Held-off / Bypass hours? Hot water is only at risk of overheating for a few midday hours (water ≥ 72 °C or PCM ≥ 62 °C). Outside that, running the pump whenever the sun gives net gain is already the right thing to do, so the controller only steps in for a few hours per day. IDLE and BYPASS both switch the pump off; BYPASS marks the cases that protect against overheating.

What "efficient" means here. Both the controller and an always-on pump collect nearly all available heat. The gain is avoiding overheat guard hits (wasted heat dumped by the safety shield and thermal stress on tank and PCM) while the same hot water is delivered. The scorecard compares both on the identical weather window.

Playback = pressing ▶ steps through the window one simulated hour at a time; drag the slider or click the colour strip to jump to any hour.
